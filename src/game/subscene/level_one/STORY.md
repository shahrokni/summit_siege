## Story

A remote strategic position built around a massive pyramid-like structure has come under attack.

Hostile forces are moving into the area from the surrounding outposts and advancing toward the summit. The position is important enough that losing it would give the enemy control over the entire sector.

The player takes the role of a sniper operating from a helicopter circling above the battlefield.

From the air, the sniper has a clear view of the conflict below: enemy soldiers moving between structures, taking cover behind obstacles, climbing toward the summit, and engaging the helicopter from the ground. Enemy vehicles equipped with missiles add an even greater threat to the aircraft.

The mission is to hold the position while the helicopter remains in the area and prevent the attacking force from taking control of the summit.

As the battle continues, the situation becomes increasingly dangerous. Ground fire intensifies, enemy vehicles engage the helicopter, and the aircraft begins taking damage.

Eventually, the helicopter is hit badly enough that the pilot can no longer keep it airborne.

The aircraft loses control and crashes into the battlefield.

The sniper survives.

The aerial advantage is gone, the battle is still ongoing, and the player is now trapped on the ground inside the combat zone.

**Level Two begins here.**
